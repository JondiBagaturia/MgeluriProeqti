SPHEREx Blink

Blink NASA SPHEREx epochs at any sky position to spot moving or variable objects. Inspired by WiseView.

Search area

Large field of view. This will be slower.

Detectors

Caps exposures combined per epoch (fewer = faster to fetch, slightly noisier). Low is about 5, Mid about 15, High is all available, spread evenly across the epoch's time span either way.

Display

Asinh uses fixed levels (median / 99.8th percentile, computed across the whole loaded sequence). The black and white sliders are inactive in this mode.

Measurements

Light curve

Flux at the target pixel, per frame. Not aperture photometry; see README.

Spectrum

Click a pixel in the image for flux vs wavelength at that exact position, across all 6 detectors. Forced photometry at one pixel, not PSF-fitted. Slow: it re-queries IRSA for that position. Compare with IRSA's official Spectrophotometry Tool.
