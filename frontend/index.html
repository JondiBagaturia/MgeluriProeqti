Display

Asinh uses fixed levels (median / 99.8th percentile, computed across the whole loaded sequence). The black and white sliders are inactive in this mode.

Measurements

Light curve

Aperture flux at the target's predicted position per frame, mJy ±1σ, for the detector shown. Hollow markers: measurement refused (masked, saturated or off-image). Error bars are statistical only: frames of a constant star differ by about 3–5% (measured on field stars). Each frame also samples a different wavelength within the detector (see λ in the frame label), so differences can be spectral rather than variability.

Spectrum

Click in the image for aperture flux (mJy, ±1σ, r = 9″, no aperture correction) vs wavelength, across all 6 detectors. Clicking on the crosshair of a proper-motion target follows its motion per exposure. Points are coloured by date. SPHEREx does not see all wavelengths at once, so a variable star's points from different dates are not one spectrum. Slow: it re-queries IRSA. Compare with IRSA's official Spectrophotometry Tool.

Each statistic is a test against "no change" with the stated errors (statistical + a 2.5 % per-exposure systematic floor measured on field stars), compared with stationary field stars in the same field when there are enough. A p-value is not the probability that the source is variable; "significant" describes the measurement, not its physical cause.
